\section*{Conclusion}

The simulated panel displays a positive value effect that is stronger among small firms and remains significant with year and industry controls and firm-clustered standard errors. Profitability is also positively associated with subsequent returns. These patterns are consistent with familiar asset-pricing intuition, but simulated data cannot independently validate a real-world return premium. The Brexit results favour UK sales over listing status as a measure of operating exposure, showing why the economic meaning of a treatment definition matters for the conclusion.
