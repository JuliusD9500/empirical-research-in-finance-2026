\section*{Abstract}

We examine valuation and one-year-ahead excess stock returns in a simulated panel of German listed firms. Higher book-to-market ratios are associated with higher returns, consistent with the Fama--French value pattern, and this relationship is stronger among small firms. It remains positive after including year and industry controls and clustering standard errors by firm. In the accompanying Brexit event study, UK sales exposure identifies substantially more negative abnormal returns, whereas UK listing status does not distinguish the groups' reactions.
