\section*{Data}

I would use a quarterly panel of US-domiciled, US-listed firms from 2022Q1 to 2025Q4. Restricting firms to December fiscal year ends aligns their reporting periods. I would exclude 2024Q3, when Helene occurred, leaving ten pre-storm quarters and five full post-storm quarters. Quarterly data separate the periods before and after the shock and allow me to examine whether any response persists. Starting in 2022 avoids the initial pandemic disruption, although later macroeconomic shocks still need to be considered.

Accounting data would come from Compustat North America through WRDS, subject to obtaining access. R\&D investment would equal 100 times quarterly research and development expense (\texttt{XRDQ}) divided by total assets (\texttt{ATQ}) at the end of 2023. Cash holdings would equal 100 times cash and short-term investments (\texttt{CHEQ}) divided by the same fixed asset base. Fixing the denominator prevents changes in reported assets after Helene from mechanically changing either ratio. Both outcomes are measured in percentage points of pre-storm assets.

I would focus on firms reporting R\&D before Helene and exclude financials (SIC 6000 to 6999) and utilities (SIC 4900 to 4999). Missing R\&D would remain missing unless company filings explicitly establish a zero value. I would require positive assets at the end of 2023 and compare both outcomes on observations where each is available. Firms that later leave the market would remain for available periods. Extreme ratios would be checked against filings, with sensitivity to winsorisation at the 1st and 99th percentiles reported.

Exposure would be based on FEMA's county designations for Individual Assistance following Helene. $E_i$ would equal one if a firm's pre-storm headquarters lies in a designated county, and zero otherwise. I would verify historical headquarters addresses using SEC filings rather than assume that current Compustat addresses were valid in 2024. Other mainland firms would form the comparison group, after checking industry composition and prior outcome trends. This definition measures headquarters exposure and does not establish damage to every firm's operations.
