\section*{Expected Outcome}

My main hypothesis ($H_1$) is that firms headquartered in affected counties reduce R\&D investment after Helene relative to other firms. I also expect a relative increase in cash holdings, which would provide complementary evidence about their financial response.

The rationale is that uncertain future revenues and urgent repair costs may make firms more reluctant to commit funds to new research projects. R\&D often creates intangible assets that are difficult to pledge as collateral. Cash, in contrast, can cover several immediate needs. Managers may therefore postpone new projects or research hiring while preserving liquidity.

The prediction is not certain. Established research programmes can be costly to interrupt, and affected firms may spend existing cash on repairs. This makes the direction of both outcomes an empirical question rather than a necessary consequence of the storm.
