\section*{Extensions and Limitations}

One limitation is that headquarters may be far from laboratories and other operating sites. Exposure can therefore be misclassified, and the resulting bias need not always reduce the estimated effect. Local demand changes, damage and financing decisions may also affect both outcomes. Their joint movement alone cannot identify precautionary behaviour.

As an extension, I would examine whether responses differ with initial financing capacity. A dummy would identify firms with end-2023 assets below the sample median and debt/assets above the median. This is a proxy for financing constraints, not a direct measure. I would add its interaction with $E_i \times \mathrm{Post}_t$, together with all lower-order terms not absorbed by fixed effects. A more negative R\&D response for this group would be consistent with financing frictions, although it would not establish the cash-preservation mechanism.
